# Evidence

Enclosure treats evidence as a product output, not a confident sentence written
after the work. These pages separate directly inspectable facts, dated runtime
observations, and conclusions drawn from them.

- [The gang uses its own machinery](dogfooding.md) records the first public
  evidence: Enclosure, Mermaiden, Modwire, and Sirenity work under
  Enclosure-registered instructions.

Broader case studies need their own bounded evidence. They should identify what
Enclosure governed, when it was used, which facts are publicly inspectable, and
where recollection or inference begins.
